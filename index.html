The retention feature

Your memory has a deadline. We keep the calendar.

Forgetting is not a character flaw, it is a schedule. JurisMind models the decay curve of every topic you study and puts the review back in front of you on the day it starts to slip.

  • Every topic has a memory score

    Each concept carries a mastery score that moves every time you answer — up when you are right, down when you guess.

  • Reviews are scheduled automatically

    Short intervals at first, then wider: 1 day, 3 days, 7, 14, 30. You revise less and keep more.

  • Weak topics surface themselves

    The topics under 60% are pulled into a coral "attention" list, so no gap stays invisible until the exam.

Evidence

Why does spaced retrieval work?

Spaced retrieval works because active recall strengthens memory and returning to material after a delay makes that recall durable. Dunlosky et al. (2013) rated practice testing and distributed practice as high-utility learning techniques, while Cepeda et al. (2006) found that the best gap between study sessions grows with the time you want to remember something.

What does the research mean for law revision?

  • Answer before rereading. A case question forces retrieval; immediate feedback corrects the answer.
  • Return after a short delay. Reviews at 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days spread practice across the semester.
  • Let errors reshuffle the queue. A miss brings the topic back tomorrow; a correct answer moves it further out.

How does the JurisMind loop compare with cramming?

Study habits, research findings and the corresponding JurisMind feature
Study habitWhat the research findsHow JurisMind responds
One long rereading sessionRereading feels productive but does not consistently improve durable learning (Dunlosky et al., 2013).Each topic becomes a short case question with an explanation after the answer.
Cramming close to the examSpacing study episodes improves later recall; the optimal gap grows with the target retention interval (Cepeda et al., 2006).The scheduler starts with a one-day review and expands to 3, 7, 14 and 30 days.
Testing only to grade yourselfRetrieval tests can improve long-term retention more than extra study (Roediger & Karpicke, 2006).Every answer updates a mastery score and recalculates the next review date.

FAQ

What do law students ask about JurisMind?

Is JurisMind a summary of the law?

No. JurisMind is a practice and retention tool. Your course remains the reference; we make sure you still remember it in week twelve.

Which law is covered?

The core of countries in the civil-law tradition: contract, commercial, labour, corporate and administrative law. Exercises stay at doctrine level so they travel between courses.

Do I need to create an account?

This demo runs entirely in your browser: your answers, streak and mastery scores are stored locally on your device.

Does it work right-to-left?

Yes. Arabic gets a full RTL layout, an Arabic typeface and its own content — not a mirrored afterthought.

Diagnostic, one weak topic, one review scheduled. That is the whole loop.
